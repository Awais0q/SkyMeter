FAQ
📝
About App
⚙️ How does the thermometer work?▾
The app uses your phone's built-in temperature sensor (if available) for indoor readings, and weather API data for outdoor temperature.
🌡️ Why is the indoor temperature not accurate?▾
Phone sensors can be affected by device heat, case, or direct sunlight. Use Scale Calibration in Settings to adjust.
☁️ Why is the outdoor temperature not accurate?▾
Outdoor data comes from weather stations and models. Differences of 1-2° are normal depending on your exact location.
📍 Why do I need to grant location permission?▾
Location is required to show accurate local weather, radar, and air quality for your area.
°C How to switch between °C and °F?▾
Use the °C / °F toggle on the main screen top bar, or go to Settings → Temperature.
Subscription
📢 How can I remove ads from the app?▾
Upgrade to Premium in Settings to remove ads and unlock all features.
👑 Can I access Premium features for free?▾
Premium features require a subscription. Free users can still use core weather data.
